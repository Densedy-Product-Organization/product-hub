**Current Version:** 0.1.0

# Vision

Build an enterprise-grade, AI-assisted Product Information Management (PIM) platform that becomes the trusted source of product information for organizations while serving as the first application built on Platform Core.

# Roadmap Principles

- Solve one business problem exceptionally well.
- Maintain low operational cost.
- AI remains optional.
- Business rules take precedence over AI.
- Enterprise architecture over shortcuts.
- Build only validated capabilities.
- Customer value drives prioritization.

---

# Success Criteria

The roadmap is successful when

- The MVP is delivered.
- The first pilot customer is onboarded.
- The first paying customer is acquired.
- Product-market fit is validated.
- Product Hub becomes the foundation for future products.
