# Product Hub MVP Scope

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** Product Discovery  
**Product Stage:** Pre-development  

## Purpose

The Product Hub MVP exists to validate whether organizations will adopt and pay for a focused product-information application that replaces fragmented spreadsheets and disconnected product records with one trusted repository.

The MVP must solve one primary business problem:

> Organizations cannot reliably manage product information because product records, classifications, attributes, descriptions, and supporting files are distributed across disconnected systems and documents.

The MVP must provide enough value for an external pilot without attempting to become a complete enterprise Product Information Management suite.

## MVP Outcome

A pilot organization must be able to:

1. Create an organization workspace.
2. Invite or register authorized users.
3. Define a recursive product taxonomy.
4. Define product attribute structures.
5. Create and maintain products.
6. Represent product models and variants.
7. Import existing product data from CSV.
8. identify invalid or incomplete data.
9. Review optional AI suggestions.
10. Search and filter product records.
11. Export corrected product data.
12. Review basic audit history.

## Primary Pilot Workflow

The MVP will prioritize this end-to-end workflow:

```text
Create organization
→ define taxonomy
→ define attributes
→ import product spreadsheet
→ validate imported records
→ review AI suggestions
→ correct product information
→ search and review products
→ export clean product data
